Debt:

Technical debt is well established concept in software with rather well understood tradeoffs.

With AI two other forms of debt are getting more and more relevant to consider:

- Cognitive debt

    The erosion of a team's shared understanding of how a system works.

- Intent debt

    Absence of documented rationale regarding why the system was built that way.
